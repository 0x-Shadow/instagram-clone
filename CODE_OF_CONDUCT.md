# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (Summary)

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and
focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes
harassment, trolling, insults, publishing others' private information, and
any conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

**Enforcement:** violations may lead to warnings, temporary bans, or permanent
removal from the project at maintainer discretion. Reports are handled
confidentially — contact the maintainer via a private security advisory or
email rather than a public issue.
